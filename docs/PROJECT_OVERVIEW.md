# LifeOS Project Overview

## Summary

LifeOS is a personal web app designed to help manage daily life from one clean dashboard. It combines tasks, habits, finance tracking, journaling, life admin, groceries, business tracking, documents, and AI-powered insights.

The goal is not to track everything for the sake of tracking. The goal is to simplify decision-making and answer:

> What needs my attention today?

## Core Purpose

LifeOS should help the user:

- Know what to focus on today
- Track spending and find savings
- Build better habits
- Capture thoughts, tasks, and ideas quickly
- Remember important life admin dates
- Organise documents and receipts
- Plan groceries and reduce takeaway spending
- Track business ideas, customers, quotes, and follow-ups
- Review life weekly and spot repeating patterns

## Product Principle

Keep the app simple, calm, and useful.

Avoid giant dashboards full of noise. Each screen should answer one clear question.

## MVP Goal

The first version should be useful every day, even without advanced AI or integrations.

The MVP should include:

1. Login
2. Home dashboard
3. Tasks
4. Brain dump
5. Habits
6. Journal
7. Basic finance tracker
8. Life admin reminders

## Long-Term Vision

LifeOS should become a personal operating system that combines data, reminders, reflection, and AI to simplify life.

Eventually it should be able to answer questions like:

- What should I focus on tomorrow?
- Where did I waste money this week?
- What habit is slipping?
- What bill or renewal is coming up?
- What groceries should I buy this week?
- What business follow-up am I forgetting?
- What patterns keep repeating in my journal?
